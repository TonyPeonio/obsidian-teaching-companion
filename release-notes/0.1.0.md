First release.

- **Rubrics as notes**: a rubric is a markdown table (`Criterion | Points | Description`, with optional level rows or one small table per criterion) under `Teaching/Rubrics/`, or, with no table at all, an outline as pasted from a syllabus: top-level bullets or headings carrying `(20 points)` are the criteria, nested bullets the description, nested items starting with a number the score levels, and a sentence like "10 points for overall quality" a criterion of its own. **New rubric** writes a table template.
- **Grade a submission**: a note, `.txt` or `.pdf` in the vault, or pasted text, is graded against a rubric by your local Ollama model. Per criterion: a proposed score within the allowed points, a one-sentence justification and up to three verbatim quotes with their page or paragraph. Quotes that are not actually in the submission are dropped and counted; out-of-range scores are clamped and flagged. The result is a draft note with an empty "Your score" column, draft comments to the student (no scores in them) and a banner saying the scores are proposals.
- **Grade a folder**: every submission in a folder, one note each, with progress and Cancel; existing notes are skipped unless you choose re-grade, and typed scores are carried over.
- **Grade book**: per rubric, a table of proposed and final totals, per-criterion averages, a distribution by 10% band and the most-missed criteria. Deterministic; rewrites only that note.
- **Rubric check**: flags criteria without a description and points that do not add up, then asks the model about overlap and unobservable descriptions; suggestions go under `## Suggestions` in the rubric note.
- Student work goes only to the Ollama URL in settings (localhost by default). No accounts, no telemetry.
